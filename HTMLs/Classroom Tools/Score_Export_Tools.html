Combine score exports

Bring together the CSV score files collected by you and your paras, then copy their rows into your shared Excel Score Log table.

  1. Use Download Score CSV on each completed assessment report.
  2. Select one or more of those CSV files below. Identical Record IDs are included once.
  3. Choose Copy Excel Rows, then paste into the next empty row of the shared Score Log table. The copied rows do not include another header.

Student entries are processed in this page's memory. This tool does not upload them, automatically save them, or change a shared OneDrive/Excel file. Copying or downloading exports is your choice. Close or clear the page when finished. New dates, students, and goal revisions are recorded as new rows.

      How this fits changing months and IEP goals

      Keep all new results in the same Score Log table. Date identifies the month; Student identifies the student; Goal ID and Goal Version identify the target being monitored. When an IEP goal changes, enter a new goal revision instead of combining different targets under one unexplained trend. Your existing monthly worksheets can remain alongside the log.

      Do not paste the same export twice into Excel. Combining the CSV files here removes duplicates within the selected files; it cannot inspect rows already pasted in your shared workbook.
