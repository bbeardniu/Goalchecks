Teacher guide · v01.0006

Small steps. Clear progress.

Choose a skill and level, collect a short check under consistent conditions, then keep the score and response details together. The site supports classroom goal checks, home practice, and clear results for your existing recordkeeping routine.

Start a check

  1. Open the skill from the homepage. Use its level or task choices to match the student's current target.
  2. Choose Baseline, Weekly Check, or Practice. There are no student, goal, or export forms to complete.
  3. Press Start check when the student and administrator are ready. A fresh form is ready automatically; open Optional: repeat a form only when you want to reuse a particular Form ID.
  4. Use the page's response controls. The one-minute chime is a cue, and the student may continue beyond it. Stop the check when the intended observation is complete.
  5. Review the report. Snip the compact results into the student's OneNote page or use Print / Save PDF. Enter the score manually in your usual shared Excel document.

Administration details that matter

  • Oral sound skills: an adult presents the spoken task and marks the response. Keep the adult prompt area out of the student's view when it would reveal the answer.
  • Spelling: the adult sees the target and presents it aloud. Do not let the student read the target word while spelling it.
  • Reading: note whether the adult read the passage or question aloud. Listening comprehension and independent reading are different conditions.
  • Reading Steps: each of the five skill choices has 100 questions from 100 original short passages. The mixed check rotates through all five skills. Use the same skill and reading support for weekly comparisons; select a new Form ID for a new randomized order.
  • Story Retell: choose a three-event or four-event story, independent reading or adult read-aloud, and spoken or typed retell. After the student finishes, rate main characters/topic, key events/details, order, and ending/main point. Each is Met or Not yet. The score remains Pending until all four are rated. The report keeps the original story, retell notes, passage-access conditions, and reading-plus-retell time; teacher grading time is excluded. This rubric has no first-minute rate.
  • Supported writing: choosing a word or arranging supplied tiles is supported sentence work. Keep its score separate from independent composition.
  • Independent writing: the result stays Pending until the teacher explicitly rates all four rubric criteria. A Pending result is not a zero. Keep the writing sample with its score.
  • Sentence order: reviewed alternatives can be accepted. The teacher can approve another grammatical arrangement without creating a second attempt. Formal checks use one check per sentence; Practice can include retries.
  • Fonts, pictures, prompts, and help: record the support used. Keep it consistent across observations intended to be compared.

One-minute fluency reads

Choose the passage, student, and read/day before starting. Mark errors with the existing right-click or keyboard tools and select the last word read. The report keeps the actual total time and the whole-read rate.

To retain a separate first-minute score when continuing longer, mark the word reached at the one-minute chime and use the page's Save minute mark control. That records the selected endpoint for the minute; a later whole-read endpoint can then be different. If a minute endpoint was not saved, treat the first-minute field as unavailable rather than guessing it from the final rate.

Keep your existing records

Use the compact result cards as your record of the student's responses and the seconds each response took. The date, score, attempts, and total time remain on the report for screenshots and printing.

  1. Take a screenshot with the Windows snipping tool and save it in the student's OneNote page.
  2. Enter the score manually in the shared Excel sheet your team already uses.
  3. Keep the skill, level, testing conditions, and date with each observation so you can compare like checks over time.

There are no Excel export controls, CSV downloads, roster forms, or spreadsheet connection steps. Keep records in your existing school-approved locations. The site does not save results after the page is closed.
