# Beard's Classroom storybook illustrations

Revision: v01.0003

Original artwork generated for this site with the built-in image-generation tool. No stock illustration downloads or external image services are used by these activities. The original Bitmoji and functional assessment diagrams remain separate from this collection.

## Art direction and prompt recipe

Children's picture-book vignettes in warm watercolor and gouache with delicate colored-pencil outlines, natural proportions, expressive faces, fine paper texture, gentle light, and a warm ivory background. Clear everyday objects and actions appropriate for elementary students. Diverse children with varied skin tones and hairstyles. Avoid emoji, flat clipart, plastic 3D surfaces, neon colors, surreal details, branding, watermarks, and readable text.

The scene-specific prompts are recorded in the `tiles` descriptions in `manifest.json`. Four-by-four sheets keep each vignette within a separate square. The sea-creature and reward sheet uses transparency. The harbor illustration is a standalone square scene.

## Files and mappings

- `storybook-01.webp` through `storybook-14.webp`: 224 painted vignettes.
- `storybook-tools.webp`: 16 transparent sea-creature and reward illustrations.
- `ship-harbor.webp`: a ship docked at a harbor pier.
- `manifest.json`: editable scene descriptions and mappings for all 300 writing prompts.
- `manifest.js`: the same prompt mappings for offline-compatible script loading.
- `storybook-art.js`: accessible illustration rendering and local asset resolution.

Images use WebP encoding for efficient loading without changing the composition. The activities preserve their existing questions, scores, timers, and reports.
