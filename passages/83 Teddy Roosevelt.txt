Teddy Roosevelt
President Teddy Roosevelt believed that nature was an important part of the United States. He believed that people should care for the animals, plants, trees, and land in nature. President Roosevelt's dream was that nature would always be there to enjoy.
As President of the United States, Teddy Roosevelt worked for laws to make this happen. These laws gave the United States parks that millions of people enjoy each year. Because Teddy Roosevelt loved animals, people call stuffed toy bears "teddy bears."
