The Library of Congress
The nation's library is called the Library of Congress. It began in 1800 with 152 books. Today, though, the Library of Congress is the world's largest library. It has more than 100,000,000 books, maps, tapes, pictures, and other things.
People don't have to visit the Library of Congress to use it. They can use a computer to see things in the Library of Congress. A computer can show people some of the books, maps, and pictures in our nation's library.
