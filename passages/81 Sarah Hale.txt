Sarah Hale
When Sarah Hale was a child more than 200 years ago, Thanksgiving was not a national holiday in the United States. At that time, most women did not have jobs. Sarah Hale was one woman who did have a job. She was a writer.
Sarah Hale believed that Thanksgiving was an important day. She believed that it should be a national holiday. For 26 years, Sarah Hale wrote and spoke about this dream. At last, in 1867, Thanksgiving became a national holiday.
