The National Park Service
Most people think of parks as places with trees and grass. Parks have other things, too. The National Park Service takes care of special parks in America. Some national parks have trees and grass. Some national parks also have caves, swamps, and sand dunes.
Besides parks, the National Park Service takes care of places and things that were important in America's past, like ships, trains, and even schools. Every state has places that are special to America.
