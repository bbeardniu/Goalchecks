People From Many Places
Most of the people who live in Mexico were born there. Most of the people in Mexico have parents who were born there, too. Many people who live in the United States, however, were born in another country. Many people also have parents who were born in another country.
For many years, people came to the United States to be able to vote. Today, people in many countries can vote. Still, many people come to live in the United States.
