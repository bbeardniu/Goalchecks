The U.S. Postal Service
You may never go to some of the places that Americans share because they are far from your home. However, one place that Americans share is close to where most people live. It's the U.S. Postal Service. 
Most days, someone from the U.S. Postal Service brings mail to mailboxes at people's homes. These mailboxes are small parts of the U.S. Postal Service. Even though they are very small, mailboxes are places that everyone in America shares.
