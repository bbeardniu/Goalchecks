Charles Drew
When Charles Drew was young, it was hard for African Americans to become doctors. Charles Drew, who was African American, did not let that stop him. He worked hard, and he finally became a doctor.
Charles Drew also worked hard to find the answer to an important problem. He found a way to keep blood fresh. When blood can be kept fresh, it can be used to make people well. Because of Charles Drew's dream, millions of lives are saved every year.
