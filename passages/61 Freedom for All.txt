Freedom for All
America's first leaders had the idea that everyone would be free and could vote. However, their idea meant that only people who looked like the leaders were free and could vote. No leaders were women. It took almost 150 years before women could vote.
No leaders were African American either. For almost the first 100 years of the new country, many African Americans were slaves. Today, all Americans can vote. Today, the idea "freedom for all" means "freedom for all people."
